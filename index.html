Practical caveats before you drive

  • Accident history is based on CARFAX inventory and exact VIN pages visible during the June 8, 2026 refresh. Any listing marked accident, minor damage, major damage, branded title, salvage, rebuilt, or not-actual-mileage is excluded from this dashboard.
  • The first link on every card is an exact VIN page from CARFAX, a dealer-owned detail page, or a manufacturer CPO inventory page, not a generic marketplace search. The June 8 run could not complete the required browser audit because the in-app browser blocked CARFAX and dealer-domain navigation; the active cars are therefore labeled source-checked, not browser-verified.
  • The dashboard now has 13 Great Value source-checked matches. It is intentionally not padded beyond vehicles that pass the live-link, panoramic-roof, clean-history, mileage, budget, resale, market-value, and comfort checks.
  • The default mileage cap is 45,000 miles. Cars above that are excluded unless you intentionally raise the rule in a future refresh.
  • Rear comfort is prioritized over flashy trims. Pilot, Telluride, and MDX are the roomiest family picks; RDX and CX-50 remain good choices only if three people fit comfortably across the second row.
  • Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Acura, Kia Telluride, and Mazda CX-50-style candidates were prioritized. Palisade, Nautilus, Pathfinder, CX-90, rental-heavy, multiple-use, damaged, over-budget, over-mileage, weak-resale, standard/small-sunroof, stale-source, Good Value, and Fair Value candidates are removed from the active dashboard.
  • Roof proof is intentionally strict. Each card includes an official trim/spec source for the panoramic roof; standard small sunroofs are not treated as a match.
  • 5-year costs are modeled estimates using listing price, EPA fuel economy, expected depreciation, insurance, maintenance/repair risk, and Texas-area driving assumptions. Your financing, insurance, and fuel costs will vary.
